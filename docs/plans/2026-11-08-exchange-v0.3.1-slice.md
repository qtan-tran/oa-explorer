## Corpus decisions implemented (with tests)

1. **Published ≠ promulgated.** `publication_linked` derives only from
   a `published` claim; `promulgation_linked` only from `promulgated`.
   Synthetic CO Resolution 0777 model: `publication_linked`, signed
   2022-08-03, Diario Oficial 52.243 published 2022-12-09
   (`issuer_attested`, gazette-mirror caveat), effective provisionally
   2022-12-09. Synthetic BRIN Regulation 12/2023 model:
   `promulgation_linked` from a year-precision `promulgated` claim.
   Disagreeing event dates: both preserved (note), effective chosen only
   from the explicit trigger; unclear trigger → validation error.
2. **Public archive link** = `verified_bytes` snapshot matching
   `archived_url` + link-level rights + passed review gate, all required
   (`public_archive_link()`). Bare `archived_url` or `url_only` snapshots
   stay internal (warning at validation, never in public views).
3. **Score actor/action** = policy actor / evaluated action, never the
   analyst; null for mixed clauses with `source_scope` and
   `basis_provision_ids` retaining detail (valued observation naming an
   actor with no basis → warning). No numeric ten-score import, no
   composite until labels, scale anchors and a codebook version are
   supplied.
4. **Unknown-start cohorts** are not negative-infinity starts and are
   excluded from definite date-filtered claims
   (`cohorts_with_definite_start()`); `cohort_end_kind()` distinguishes
   `unbounded_supported` (null end + `not_applicable`) from `unknown`
   (null end + `unknown`/`unreviewed`); null end with a valued status is
   an error.
5. **UPTC Agreement 2/2026 lead** (`V-CO-UPTC-2-2026`):
   `attested_unrecovered` + `announcement_attested`; adoption 2026-01-29
   issuer-attested via news only; no verified PDF/annex, effective date,
   physical clause or hash. Excluded from `comparison_eligible_versions()`
   and from public views even when the release flag is flipped.
